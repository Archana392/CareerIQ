# CareerIQ

## AI-Powered Career Intelligence

CareerIQ is an AI-powered career intelligence and employability platform designed to help students and job seekers understand their current capabilities, identify skill gaps, improve their resumes, discover relevant career opportunities, and become job-ready.

Instead of simply asking users to apply for jobs, CareerIQ creates a personalized journey:

**Where am I now? → What do I need? → How do I improve? → Which opportunities fit me?**

---

## Problem

Students and job seekers often struggle to understand:

- Whether their resume is ATS-friendly
- Which skills they are missing for their desired role
- What skills companies currently expect
- Which jobs actually match their profile
- What they should learn next
- Whether they are truly job-ready
- How to continuously improve their employability

Recruiters also spend significant time manually reviewing resumes and comparing candidates against job requirements.

CareerIQ brings these processes together into one intelligent platform.

---

## Solution

CareerIQ uses AI-driven career intelligence to analyze a user's profile and provide personalized recommendations.

The platform connects:

**Resume → Skills → Skill Gaps → Learning → Career Path → Job Matching → Job Readiness**

For recruiters:

**Job Requirements → Candidate Matching → Candidate Intelligence → Readiness Evaluation**

---

## Key Features

### 1. AI Resume Analyzer

Users can upload their resume in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format and receive an intelligent analysis.

The system provides:

- ATS compatibility score
- Resume strength score
- Detected skills
- Missing skills
- Recommended skills
- Keyword optimization
- Experience relevance
- Role alignment
- Resume improvement suggestions

---

### 2. Personalized Career Path

Users select their desired professional role and receive a personalized career roadmap based on:

- Current skills
- Resume information
- Skill gaps
- Industry requirements
- Job requirements
- Learning progress

The roadmap follows:

**Current Skills → Skill Development → Projects → Resume Improvement → Job Readiness**

---
